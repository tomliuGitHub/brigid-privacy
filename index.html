Privacy Policy — Brigid

For the app “Bridge Coach – Brigid” on the Apple App Store and Google Play (Apple bundle ID com.wedoapp.Brigid; Google Play package com.wedoapp.brigidbridge).
Last updated: 31 July 2026.

This policy explains what information the Brigid app (“Brigid”, “the app”, “we”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Brigid you agree to this policy.

Accounts

You do not need to register, or give us your name, email address, or any similar personal identifier, to use Brigid. Your activity is associated with an app‑generated identifier so we can save your progress and provide the coaching features. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, depending on where you downloaded the app; we never see or store your payment card details.

Information we collect

  • Practice and gameplay data — the deals you bid and play, your bids and results, and the progress and accuracy statistics shown in the app. We store this so your progress and ranks persist across sessions.
  • Coach interactions — the messages you send to the Personal Coach and the related hands or questions. To generate a response, this content is sent to a third‑party AI service provider (OpenAI). It is used to produce the coaching reply and is not used by us to identify you.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription, provided to us by Apple App Store or Google Play billing.
  • Technical and diagnostic data — limited information such as app version, device type, chosen language, and error/diagnostic logs, used to operate and troubleshoot the app.

Brigid is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).

How we use information

  • To provide core features — bidding and board practice, progress tracking, ranks, and the Personal Coach.
  • To generate coaching responses to your questions.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To manage subscriptions and provide support.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Brigid contains no ads. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Apple App Store and Google Play — app distribution and subscription billing.
  • OpenAI — processes Personal Coach messages to generate replies.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers — store gameplay and coach data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the service.

Data security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We retain your gameplay and coach data for as long as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your data at any time (see below).

Requesting data deletion

To request deletion of the data associated with your use of Brigid (“Bridge Coach – Brigid”), follow these steps:

  • Email wedoappinc@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my data.”
  • So we can locate your data, send the request from the device you use for Brigid, or include any in-app identifier the app shows you.
  • We process deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email.

What is deleted: your gameplay and practice history, your progress and statistics, and your Personal Coach conversations.
What may be kept: records we are required to retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons, and anonymized or aggregated data that no longer identifies you.

Your choices and rights

You may also request access to the data associated with your app identifier by contacting us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; we will honor applicable requests.

Changes to this policy
